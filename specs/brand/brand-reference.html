Favicon / app-icon mark

The mark is inherently monochrome — the source badge has never had a colour version, so there is no "full colour" lockup to invent. Use mono-white on any dark surface (contrast of black-on-ink-950 is ~1.07:1 — effectively invisible); mono-black reads at 21:1 on white/light surfaces. The favicon mark drops the wordmark and script (illegible under ~48px) and keeps only the badge's outer silhouette.

Clear space

X = the cap-height of the "S" in SANCHEZ (≈ a third of the full badge's height). Keep clear space of at least X on every side before any text, edge or other logo touches the mark. Derived from the redrawn geometry, not an arbitrary pixel value.

Already wired up as --font-display / --font-text in prototype/styles.css. Both are OFL-licensed Google Fonts — free for self-hosted commercial web use, no licence risk. The logo's own hand-lettered wordmark is a separate, one-off mark; Barlow Condensed is the site's display face, not a replacement for the custom lettering in the badge itself.

Do / Don't

Do

  • Preserve the mark's proportions — scale uniformly only
  • Use mono-white on dark surfaces, mono-black on light
  • Keep at least one X of clear space on every side
  • Use the favicon mark below ~120px instead of shrinking the full lockup
  • Use the approved origin line exactly as written

Don't

  • Stretch, skew or rotate the mark
  • Recolour it outside the approved black/white variants
  • Add glows, drop shadows or bevels
  • Redraw the letters, script or gloves independently of the master file
  • Introduce new iconography (stars, crowns, fists, laurels…)
  • Describe the products as "Australian made"
