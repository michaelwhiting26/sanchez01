The owner's current word on how each part of the site behaves. Anyone changing these parts (human or agent) reads this first and does not undo a line here without the owner saying so. Newest decision wins; when one changes, edit the line (do not append a contradicting rule elsewhere).

## Home is a plain shop (owner, 7 Oct 2026)
- **The site is a plain shop on the pattern of Infinitude Fight.** The owner showed Jesse the 3D store over lunch; Jesse said it felt like a video game and asked for a site "exactly like Infinitude Fight". So: a top strip, a header with "Design in 3D", a hero, a row of products each marked 3D, picture tiles for the range, three steps, a dark "fully custom" panel, a short list of what is true of every piece, a four-column footer (`app/page.tsx`, `components/shop`, `styles/shop.css`, content in `lib/shop/catalogue.ts`). `/shop` is the product grid; `/how-it-works` the three steps.
- **Home order** (owner, 8 Oct 2026; the slider and "Design in 3D" at the bottom are from 7 Oct): hero; then the craft strip "Made by hand", four pictures in this order: the maker, a finished piece, the construction up close, the bags in a gym (`SHOP_CRAFT`); then the range slider, a rail of pill-shaped stops (Everything, Gloves, Heavy bags, Focus mitts, Head guards, Groin guards, Gym fit-outs) that shows every workshop picture or only one kind (`components/shop/WorkGallery.tsx`), over "From the workshop"; technical detail (Key Features) only after the pictures; and the "Design in 3D" product row at the bottom, above the quote request. The six picture tiles ("Our range") are gone: the slider replaces them.
- **Key Features are four short, even lines** (owner, 8 Oct 2026): Precision cut, Reinforced construction, Permanent print, Master stitchwork, one sentence each (`JESSE_FEATURES`). This replaces Jesse's longer paragraphs word for word. The first three sentences say only what his wording says; the stitchwork sentence is ours and waits for his confirmation. "World class. Handmade." stays.
- **Get a Quote is a working form** (owner, 8 Oct 2026): Name, Email or Instagram, Product, What do you want made, Send (`/contact`, `components/shop/QuoteForm.tsx`, `/api/quote`, table `quote_requests`). No page tells a visitor that something "will go here". Requests are kept only where `DATABASE_URL` is set; without it the form says it cannot take requests and points to Instagram, and never claims a request was sent. Still open: who is told when a request arrives (nothing is emailed yet).
- **Only the pattern is theirs.** Every word, picture, colour and line of code is ours. Nothing of theirs is copied in, and none may be (same rule as the builders, below).
- **A product page is its 3D builder**, as on their site: "Customise now" opens `/build/<product>` (the heavy bag opens `/configure`). The builders are unchanged apart from "Back to shop".
- **The shop is light; the builders stay dark.** Shop buttons are a filled rust bar from the logo, square-cornered, with the same shape in outline for second actions. This replaces the brass pill for the shop pages only; the builders and the parked pages keep "Buttons are plain" below.
- **No price, size, delivery time, review or shipping promise is shown** until Jesse confirms it: cards read "Price to come" and step 3 says orders are not open yet. No cart or account icon until there is something to buy.
- **Pictures:** Jesse's own product photographs, cut out onto a clear background (owner, 7 Oct 2026: 36 pictures in `public/assets/products`, made by `tools/cutouts`, masters in Dropbox `assets/product-cutouts`), are used wherever one fits: the focus-mitts and groin-guard cards and the "From the workshop" row on the home page. The gloves, head guard and heavy bag cards still use the builders' own models (`tools/shop/thumbs.mjs`) and the bag render until he supplies photographs of those. **A piece lettered with a person's full name (the Freddie Roach, Bowman, Christian/Ennor, Team Savva and TTL pieces) is not shown anywhere on the site until that person gives written consent**; the files are in the folder, unused. Initials (ZV, S+H) are shown. A view or close crop of such a piece with no lettering in frame (striking face, hand opening, laced rim) may be shown (8 Oct 2026; made by `tools/cutouts/details.py`). Pictures under 600 px across (the phone screenshots and the video stills) are not used: they would look soft.
- **The 3D store is superseded, not deleted** (owner: "don't delete what we've done because I'll use that again"; "put 3D as superseded"). It runs whole at `/3d-store`, linked as "Superseded: 3D store" in the shop footer; the branch `3d-store-parked` on GitHub holds it as the home page as it stood on 7 Oct 2026 (commit `ba782ee`). Everything under "The 3D store" below still describes it and still applies to it. The scroll site before it is still at `/superseded`.
- **Jesse as the running character** (the master rule in `CLAUDE.md`) belongs to the scroll site and the 3D store. The shop has no character on it; do not add one without the owner.
- Built on the branch `infinitude-shop`, live on `main` since 7 Oct 2026 (owner's go-ahead).
