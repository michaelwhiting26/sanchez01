How every bag is made

01

Precision cutting

Every piece of Persian vinyl used to make your bag is hand cut with elite precision.

02

Reinforced adhesive

HH-66 Vinyl Cement (by RH Adhesives): a market-leading, solvent-based adhesive, engineered for a bond stronger than the vinyl itself, rapid setting, and extreme durability.

03

Silicone-based durable screen printing

High-durability silicone-based screen printing applies a flexible, permanent design directly onto the bag's surface for a long-lasting, impact-resistant finish that withstands heavy use.
